# ✨ Glow Hunt

> **Chase the light. Beat your score.**

A fast-paced neon mini-game where you control a glowing orb, collect stars and gems, avoid danger, and try to achieve your highest score.

🎮 **Play the Game:**  
https://suha-2004.github.io/glow-hunt/

---

## 🌌 About the Game

**Glow Hunt** is a simple browser-based arcade game designed around a dark neon space theme.

Move your glowing orb around the screen and collect different objects before they disappear.

⭐ Collect stars to increase your score  
💎 Collect gems for bonus points  
🔴 Avoid danger orbs  
🔥 Build combos to increase your points  
❤️ You have 3 lives  
⏱️ You have 60 seconds to get the highest score possible

Your best score is automatically saved in your browser.

---

## 🎮 How to Play

### 🖱️ Desktop

Move your mouse around the game area to control the glowing orb.

### 📱 Mobile

Touch and move your finger around the screen to control the orb.

### 🎯 Goal

Collect as many valuable objects as possible before:

- The timer reaches **0**
- You lose all **3 lives**

| 💎 Gem | +5 |
| 🔴 Danger | Lose 1 life |
